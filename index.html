Selected work

Final-year project · Phase 1 complete

Secure Wireless Transceiver on FPGA for Crew Communication

Team of four · October 2026 · Guide: Dr. C. D. Anilkumar · MATLAB · Python

The user and the problem

Small crews of fewer than ten people work in engine rooms, on open decks and around machinery. Their voice is buried in noise, the radio link can be overheard or tampered with, and existing intercoms are built for large crews or can’t be customized.

The system we’re designing

  • 2 microphones
  • NLMS noise canceller
  • AES-256-GCM
  • LDPC / Turbo coding
  • Modulator + radio

Clean the voice first, then protect it, then make it survive the channel. The target is one Xilinx Zynq chip per crew unit.

Decisions and results so far

  • Noise: chose a 64-tap NLMS filter over RLS and others because it needs only about one million operations per second, which suits an FPGA.
  • Coding: compared LDPC and Turbo in MATLAB. At 4 dB SNR, bit error rate was about 1.5×10−6 (Turbo) and 2.7×10−6 (LDPC), and the two matched at 6 and 8 dB.
  • Security: chose AES-256-GCM so every frame is encrypted and authenticated. In Python, a wrong key or an altered, missing or reordered frame is rejected.

Built product · team project

Smart Pet Feeder

Team of four · 2026 · ESP32 · ESP32-CAM · Supabase · Android app

The problem

Owners who are busy or travelling can’t be sure their pet ate, and over- or underfeeding affects health. Typical feeders run on timers and don’t confirm the pet is there or how much it ate.

What we built

  • Portions calculated from the pet’s breed, age and weight.
  • Camera-based presence detection, so food is dispensed only when the pet is there.
  • A load cell that measures what was actually eaten.
  • Cloud sync and a mobile app for schedules, history and missed-meal alerts.
